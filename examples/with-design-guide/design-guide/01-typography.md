# Typography

This page documents the type scale and inline elements you can use in the
manuscript.

## Headings

A chapter starts with an `# H1`. The shared stylesheet forces a page break
before each one. Use `## H2` for major sections and `### H3` for
sub-sections; deeper than that is unusual in a print book.

## Body text

Body paragraphs are 11pt Georgia at 1.5 line-height with justified
alignment and automatic hyphenation. Avoid double spaces between
sentences — the renderer collapses them, and the typesetter's eye will
twitch either way.

A second paragraph here demonstrates the spacing between blocks. Notice
that consecutive paragraphs share a `0.6em` bottom margin, which is just
enough to separate them visually without breaking the flow.

## Inline elements

You can use **bold** for emphasis sparingly, *italic* for titles or for
the first introduction of a term, and `inline code` for filenames,
commands, or anything that needs a monospaced face.

> Block quotes are set off with a left border and italicized body text.
> Use them for epigraphs, pull quotes, or short citations — anything
> longer than two lines belongs in the body.

## Lists

A bulleted list:

- First item
- Second item, slightly longer so you can see how a wrapped line looks
  inside the same bullet
- Third item

A numbered list:

1. First step
2. Second step
3. Third step
