# Named Page Margins

A look sets `@page full { margin: 0 }` for its plates; the manifest sets the
book's margins. The prose pages print with the manifest's margins, the plate
on the next page with none.

The west bank ledger opens with the spring survey, taken on foot at the lowest
tide of the season. Every stake line is walked twice, once at first light and
once near noon, because the morning sun flattens the mudflat's relief and
hides the drainage cuts that matter most to anyone who has to cross it later
in the year.

## After the Plate

A stake that has moved is not always a stake that has failed. Silt travels,
and a channel that ran hard against the eastern bank one winter will have
shouldered its way twenty feet west by the following autumn.
