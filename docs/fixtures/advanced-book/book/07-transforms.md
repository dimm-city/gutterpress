# Transform Drills

The duty plugin rewrites several of this chapter's constructs at render
time: marker paragraphs become component chrome, and one call-box is
rebuilt from an ordinary quotation. None of that rewriting may ever
reach the file — the lines below stay exactly as typed.

@brief

Read this chapter twice: once for the drills, once for what the desk
does to them on review. The two readings should never disagree.

@end-brief

## The reviewed copy

A reviewed chapter carries a finding from the duty officer. The finding
is set in its own panel so a shelf inspection can spot it without
reading the prose around it.

@verdict "Cleared for reprint"

## Field advisories

Advisories are written as plain quotations and dressed by the desk on
review. The dressing is presentation; the quotation is the record.

> [!TIP] Weather eye
>
> Watch the ridge, not the sky overhead. The squall you can see coming
> is the one you had time to write down.

## Counted drills

A counted drill closes on its own last step, the way wardens actually
write them — terminator on the line after the final count, no blank
line between.

@track "Ridge count"

1. Pace the north cairn line before first light.
2. Log every marker post against the district card.
@end-track

The counts above are re-checked at each quarter-day; the drill panel is
drawn by the desk, never by the author.
